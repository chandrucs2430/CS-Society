# Good Neighbor — Make Your Block Better 🌱

A community-driven platform that empowers neighbors to report issues, volunteer, and make small acts of kindness that collectively improve local neighborhoods.

---

## ✨ Vision
> *"A little good goes a long way."*

Good Neighbor encourages residents to take simple, meaningful actions that brighten their surroundings and strengthen community bonds.

---

## 🚀 Features
- **Report Issues**: Identify and log local problems (e.g., litter, broken sidewalks, overflowing bins).
- **Volunteer Tasks**: Claim and resolve issues to directly improve your block.
- **Track Impact**: See real-time stats on issues reported, resolved, and volunteer hours.
- **Community Recognition**: Earn badges and eco-points for contributions.

## 🛠️ Getting Started
1. **Sign up** to join your neighborhood block.
2. **Browse tasks** near you.
3. **Report or volunteer** to resolve issues.
4. **Track progress** and celebrate community wins.
